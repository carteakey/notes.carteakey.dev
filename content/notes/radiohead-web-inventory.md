## Official & Band-Connected

[Radiohead Public Library](https://radiohead.com/library) — full archive organised by album
[W.A.S.T.E. Headquarters](https://www.wasteheadquarters.com/) — official news, mailing list, tour info ✅ active
[W.A.S.T.E. Links Page](https://links.wasteheadquarters.com/) — band's curated hub (Radiohead, Thom, The Smile, Donwood)
[W.A.S.T.E. Store](https://store.wasteheadquarters.com/) — official merch for all band members
[Official Message Board](http://msgboard.radiohead.com) — original band-hosted board, now spam-flooded ⚠️ dead in practice

## Fan Sites & Deep Archives

[Citizen Insane](https://citizeninsane.eu/) — exhaustive database, all albums + solo projects, est. 2006 ✅ actively updated
[Green Plastic Radiohead](https://www.greenplastic.info/) — oldest US fansite, est. 1997, frozen ~2012 ✅ still online
[Green Plastic Cold Storage](https://www.greenplastic.info/cold-storage/) — print articles, fan art, facsimile of lost site "Lift", Ed's diary, W.A.S.T.E. mailing list archive ✅ still online
[At Ease](https://ateaseweb.com/) — long-running news + live archive, est. ~1997; forum dead Aug 2024 ⚠️ news pages live, board gone
[W.A.S.T.E. Central](https://wastecentral.ning.com/) — fan community blogs, semi-official Ning network ⚠️ decayed, accounts inaccessible
[Radiohead Almanac](https://the-radiohead-almanac.fandom.com/wiki/The_Radiohead_Almanac_Wiki) — fan wiki cataloguing every known live recording ✅ active project

## Scrapbooks, Blogs & Notes

[Dead Air Space](http://archive.radiohead.com/Site12/deadairspace/) — Thom Yorke's blog, In Rainbows / TKOL era
[Diet of Worms](https://radiohead.com/library) — 2004 scrapbook, via Public Library Memory Hole
[Stanley Donwood](https://www.instagram.com/stanleydonwood) — artwork, process, prints

## Live Recordings & Bootleg Archives

[Radiohead Not For Profit](https://www.radiohead-notforprofit.com) — free lossless live recordings (FLAC/SHN), show pages + setlists ✅ active
[Internet Archive — Live Hub](https://archive.org/details/RadioheadHub) — every live performance, On a Friday through TKOL ✅ active
[Essential Radiohead Bootlegs](https://theessentialradioheadbootlegs.tumblr.com) — curated guide to best recordings by era, with descriptions + links ✅ active
[Radiohead Not For Profit — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Radioheadnotforprofitofficial) — full-show audio uploads ✅ active

## Community

[r/radiohead](https://reddit.com/r/radiohead) — primary active fan community ✅ active
[ET CETERA Forum](https://etceteraforum.com) — new dedicated forum, launched 2025, spiritual successor to At Ease + Mortigi Tempo ✅ active, ~2k members

## Historical Reference Only (Wayback Machine / Dead)

[58hours.com](https://www.58hours.com) — gig database + world-map setlist tracker; intermittent ⚠️ mostly dead
[Mortigi Tempo](https://wastecentral.ning.com/) — Green Plastic's message board community; effectively offline ❌
[followmearound.com](http://followmearound.com) — fan campaign site for unreleased track; referenced in 2021 press ❌ dead
[Injektilo](https://web.archive.org/web/*/injektilo.com) — early FTP live-show host, absorbed into Green Plastic ~1999 ❌ Wayback only
[Lift](https://www.greenplastic.info/cold-storage/) — Jonathan Moore's early fansite; facsimile preserved in Green Plastic Cold Storage ❌ original gone
[Planet Telex / Wasteland / FADE OUT / Tony's Page](http://archive.radiohead.com/Site1/) — named on Site 1 links page; all gone ❌ Wayback only
